# Cancellations and Rescheduling

How to cancel or move an appointment with Tidewell Home Services (a fictional demo company).

## How to cancel a booking

You can cancel from the Tidewell app under My Bookings, or by asking our support assistant in chat with your booking reference, which looks like BK-1042. Cancelling a booking and asking for a refund are handled together: the refund amount follows the Refund Policy based on how much notice you give.

## Rescheduling an appointment

Rescheduling is free when you move the appointment at least 24 hours before the original start time. Changes made less than 24 hours before the appointment carry a $25 late change fee, because the professional has already planned their route. You can reschedule a booking up to two times. After that, please cancel and create a new booking.

## If you are not home when the professional arrives

The professional waits 15 minutes and tries to call you. If they cannot reach you, the visit is marked as a no-show. No-shows are treated like a cancellation with less than 24 hours notice, so they are not refunded.

## When Tidewell cancels

Occasionally we must cancel because of severe weather, illness, or an emergency. When that happens we notify you by text and email as early as possible, give you a full refund automatically, and offer priority rebooking.

## Arrival windows

Professionals arrive within a two hour arrival window that you choose at booking. A professional arriving within the window is on time. If they are running more than 30 minutes past the end of the window, you can cancel for a full refund regardless of notice.
