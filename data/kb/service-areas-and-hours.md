# Service Areas and Hours

Where and when Tidewell Home Services (a fictional demo company) operates.

## Cities we serve

We currently serve Austin, Dallas, Houston, and San Antonio in Texas, plus Denver, Colorado. Within each city we cover addresses up to 25 miles from downtown. Enter your ZIP code in the app to confirm coverage before booking.

## Appointment hours

Appointments are available Monday through Saturday from 8 AM to 6 PM local time. Sunday appointments are available for cleaning services only, from 10 AM to 4 PM. We are closed on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day.

## How far ahead you can book

You can book up to 60 days in advance. Same-day bookings are possible when a professional is available, as long as you book at least three hours before the start of the arrival window.

## Addresses outside the service area

If your address is outside our coverage, the app will not let you complete the booking. You can join the waitlist for your ZIP code and we will email you when service launches in your area.
