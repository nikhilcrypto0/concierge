# Our Professionals and Your Safety

How Tidewell Home Services (a fictional demo company) vets professionals and keeps visits safe.

## Background checks and vetting

Every professional passes an identity verification and a criminal background check before their first job, and the check is repeated every year. Electricians and plumbers must hold a valid state license, which we verify before activation. Professionals also complete a skills assessment and a safety training course.

## Insurance

All professionals are covered by general liability insurance of up to $1,000,000 per incident while working on a Tidewell booking.

## Knowing who is coming

The app shows your professional's first name, photo, and rating the day before your appointment. On arrival they should show you the Tidewell app with your booking open. If the person at your door does not match the profile, do not let them in and contact support right away.

## Do I need to be home

You do not need to be home for cleaning services if you leave access instructions in the booking notes. For repairs, electrical, plumbing, and AC work, an adult over 18 must be present for the whole visit.

## Pets

Please tell us about pets in the booking notes. Secure aggressive animals in a separate room during the visit. Professionals may leave and mark the visit as a no-show if they feel unsafe.

## Emergencies

Tidewell is not an emergency service. If you smell gas, see sparking wires, or have flooding you cannot stop, leave the area and call emergency services or your utility company first.
