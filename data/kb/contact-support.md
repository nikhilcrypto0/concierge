# Contacting Support

How to reach a person at Tidewell Home Services (a fictional demo company).

## Support hours

Our virtual assistant is available 24 hours a day in the app and on the website. Human support agents are available Monday through Saturday, 7 AM to 9 PM Central Time, and Sunday 9 AM to 5 PM Central Time.

## Talking to a person

Ask the assistant to connect you with a person at any time. During support hours an agent usually replies within 15 minutes. Outside support hours your conversation is queued and an agent replies first thing the next morning.

## What to have ready

Have your booking reference ready, which starts with BK, such as BK-1042. For problems with completed work, photos help us resolve the issue faster.

## Complaints and escalations

If you are not satisfied with how support handled your issue, ask for an escalation. A support lead reviews escalated cases within two business days.

## Email and phone

Email support at help@tidewell.example. Phone support is available for existing bookings during support hours. The phone number is shown in the app under Help, then Call Us.
