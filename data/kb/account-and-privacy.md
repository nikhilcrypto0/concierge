# Your Account and Privacy

Managing your Tidewell Home Services account (a fictional demo company) and how we handle your data.

## Changing your email, phone, or password

Update your email address, phone number, and password in the app under Account Settings. Changing your email sends a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect once you click it. For security, support agents cannot change your email address or password for you.

## Saved addresses and payment methods

You can save up to five addresses and three payment methods. Remove a saved card at any time under Account Settings, then Payment Methods. A card linked to an upcoming booking cannot be removed until that booking is completed or cancelled.

## Deleting your account

Request account deletion in Account Settings, then Privacy. Your account is deleted within 30 days. Upcoming bookings must be cancelled first. We keep payment and invoice records for seven years because tax law requires it.

## What data we collect and why

We collect your name, contact details, service addresses, booking history, and payment tokens. We never store full card numbers. We use this information to schedule services, process payments, and provide support. We do not sell your personal information.

## Support conversations

Chats with our support assistant are stored for 12 months to resolve disputes and improve service quality, then deleted. The assistant never asks for your password or full card number. If anyone asks for those, it is not Tidewell.
