# Payments and Billing

How paying for a Tidewell Home Services booking works (fictional demo company).

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept cash, checks, or payment directly to the professional. Paying outside the Tidewell app voids the Satisfaction Guarantee.

## When your card is charged

We place a temporary authorization when you book. Your card is charged 24 hours before the appointment starts. For jobs with a written quote, any approved extra work is charged after the job is completed.

## Receipts and invoices

A receipt is emailed as soon as your card is charged. You can also download invoices for any past booking from the Tidewell app under My Bookings, then Receipts. Business customers can add a company name and tax ID to invoices in Account Settings.

## Promo codes and credits

Enter a promo code at checkout, before you confirm the booking. Only one promo code can be used per booking, and codes cannot be added after the booking is confirmed. Tidewell credits from referrals or service recovery are applied automatically to your next booking and expire 12 months after they are issued.

## Tipping

Tips are optional and go 100% to your professional. You can add a tip in the app for up to 7 days after the service is completed.

## A charge you do not recognize

If you see a Tidewell charge you do not recognize, contact support with the date and amount. Do not dispute the charge with your bank first, because a chargeback freezes the booking and slows down any refund.
