# Services and Pricing

What Tidewell Home Services (a fictional demo company) offers and what it costs.

## Cleaning services

- Standard cleaning: $120 for homes up to 1,500 square feet, plus $30 for each additional 500 square feet. Covers kitchens, bathrooms, dusting, vacuuming, and mopping.
- Deep cleaning: $240 for homes up to 1,500 square feet. Adds inside cabinets, baseboards, oven, fridge interior, and window sills. Recommended before moving in or out.

Cleaning supplies and equipment are included. If you prefer that we use your own products, tell us in the booking notes.

## Repairs and handyman services

- Handyman visit: $95 for the first hour, then $40 per additional half hour. Covers furniture assembly, mounting TVs and shelves, and small repairs.
- Plumbing: $135 service call, which includes the first 30 minutes of work. Parts are billed at cost.
- Electrical: $150 service call with a licensed electrician, including the first 30 minutes. Parts are billed at cost.

For jobs expected to take longer than three hours, we send a written quote before work starts. You are never charged for work you did not approve.

## Air conditioning and heating

Seasonal AC tune-up costs $110 per unit and includes filter replacement, coil cleaning, and a refrigerant pressure check. Furnace inspections cost $99. Repairs beyond the tune-up are quoted separately.

## Trip fee and what is not included

There is no trip fee inside our service areas. We do not handle roofing, gas line work, pest control, or mold remediation. For a gas smell, leave the building and call your gas utility or emergency services first.
