# Satisfaction Guarantee

What Tidewell Home Services (a fictional demo company) does when a completed job is not right.

## What the guarantee covers

If a completed service does not meet the standard described in the booking, we will send a professional back to fix it at no cost. The guarantee covers cleaning that missed areas included in your service, and repairs that fail because of workmanship within 30 days.

## How to make a claim

Report the problem within 72 hours of the service being completed for cleaning, or within 30 days for repair workmanship. Contact support with your booking reference, a short description, and photos if possible. A member of our quality team reviews every claim personally, usually within one business day.

## Re-service or refund

In most cases the quality team offers a free re-service first. If a re-service is not possible or does not solve the problem, the team may approve a full or partial refund. Refunds under the guarantee are always decided by a person, never automatically.

## What is not covered

The guarantee does not cover problems caused by pre-existing damage, parts you supplied, work you asked the professional to skip, or bookings paid outside the Tidewell app.

## Damage to your property

Every professional is insured. If something in your home was damaged during a service, report it within 48 hours. Damage claims are handled by our claims team, who will contact you directly.
