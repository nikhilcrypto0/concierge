# Refund Policy

Tidewell Home Services is a fictional company used to demonstrate this project. This policy explains when a booking can be refunded and how much you get back.

## How much you get back when you cancel

The refund depends on how much notice you give before the scheduled start time of your appointment.

- 48 hours or more before the appointment: full refund of the amount you paid.
- Between 24 and 48 hours before the appointment: 50% of the booking amount.
- Less than 24 hours before the appointment: no refund. Our professional has already reserved that time for you.

If Tidewell cancels your appointment for any reason, including weather or a professional being unavailable, you always receive a full refund.

## Refunds after the service is completed

A completed service is not refunded automatically. If you are unhappy with the work, contact support within 72 hours of completion. A member of our quality team reviews every case personally and may offer a free re-service or a refund. See the Satisfaction Guarantee article for details.

## How refund requests are approved

Every refund is reviewed and approved by a member of the Tidewell support team before any money moves. You will receive a confirmation message once your refund is approved. Our virtual assistant can start a refund request for you but can never issue a refund on its own.

## When the money arrives

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Apple Pay and Google Pay refunds follow the timeline of the linked card. We cannot refund to a different card or to a bank account.

## Bookings that were already refunded

A booking can only be refunded once. If you already received a partial refund, the remaining balance is not refundable unless our quality team approves an exception.
